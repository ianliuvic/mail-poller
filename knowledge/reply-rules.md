# Reply Rules for Ian Liu

## Questions to Ask Customers
- If first contact: ask for designs/tech pack/size chart: "Could you please send us your designs and let us review them?"
- If customer wants custom: ask for detailed tech pack, size measurements, color preferences, logo details, etc.
- If sending samples: ask for address and contact information for shipping: "Please provide us with your specific address and contact information for the shipping."
- If customer interested in specific fabric: ask what fabric they want: "Please tell us what you want, then we can source it for you."
- If shipping cost: ask total quantity and destination: "Please tell us how many pcs do you need in total and then we will calculate shipping cost for you."
- If customer unclear: ask for clarification on design details.
- Follow-up emails: ask if they checked previous info, if they have questions, or if they need help: "We are wondering if you checked the information we provided, and do you have any questions or concerns on it."

## Information to Consistently Provide
- MOQ: ready-to-sell 1 pc, private label/ODM 50 pcs per color per style, custom OEM 100 pcs per color per style, special 300+.
- Sample fee: $50 USD per design (sometimes refundable if bulk order over 300 pcs). Ready samples $10-20 USD each.
- Sample lead time: 7-14 days.
- Bulk lead time: 4-6 weeks after sample approval.
- Shipping time: 7 days by air express, 30+ days by sea.
- Fabric options: 82% Nylon/18% Spandex, 85% Polyester/15% Spandex, recycled options, lining, etc.
- Certifications: OEKO-TEX, REACH.
- Payment terms: standard 30% deposit, 70% balance before shipment (or 40/60).
- Price conditions: usually EXW or FOB, sometimes DDP/DAP for small air shipments.
- Provide links to catalog, fabric guide, size guide, product page.
- Include signature with contact and website.
- Offer to source fabrics/trims, provide branding.

## Steps/CTAs Habitually Included
- Attach catalog or price list.
- Provide Google Drive link for files.
- Ask for designs/tech pack.
- Offer to send fabric swatches.
- Invite to ZOOM video call or factory visit.
- Suggest using custom system on website.
- End with "Looking forward to your reply" or "Please kindly reply".

## Things He Never/Rarely Does
- Never overpromises exact fabric match or quality without seeing samples.
- Rarely gives exact unit price without detailed design and quantity info; always qualifies.
- Never uses hard sales pressure or aggressive language.
- Never uses emojis.
- Never sends incomplete replies; always addresses all points.
- Rarely forgets to include signature block.
- Rarely uses "Dear" as greeting.

## Formatting Conventions
- Use "Hi [Name]," salutation.
- Use bullet points (dashes or numbers) for multiple answers.
- Use paragraphs for explanatory text.
- Include attachments and links.
- Always end with "Best regards," and signature.
- Use standard punctuation, no emojis.
