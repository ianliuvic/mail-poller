# Size Guide

Two kinds of measurements matter in swimwear: the flat-lay swimwear measurements we work from for production, and the body measurements used to choose a size. This page covers both.

## How we measure for production

For production we work from **flat-lay swimwear measurements** — each piece measured lying flat, in centimetres — not from body measurements. Every style is graded across a size range, and the numbers in each size column show exactly how one size moves to the next.

The tables below are a worked example from one of our standard two-piece styles (XXS–26W). They show every measurement point we use on the top and the bottom, the grading between sizes, and the tolerance we work to. If your style has its own spec sheet, we follow yours — this example simply shows what a complete flat-lay spec looks like and how we grade.

### How to measure

- **Bust** — measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the ground.

- **Underbust** — measure directly under your bust, around your ribcage.

- **Waist** — measure around the narrowest part of your natural waist.

- **Hip** — measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat.

- **Torso** — measure from the top of your shoulder, down through the crotch, and back up to the starting point.

- **Height** — stand straight without shoes and measure from the top of your head to the floor.

For reference only — these are body measurements, not swimwear measurements. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up.

## Common questions

 What measurements should I include in a tech pack?
Send the flat-lay measurements for each piece — like the example above — together with the grading across your size range. Front and back flat images or a physical sample help us confirm every measurement point before production.
  Do you follow our size chart or your own?
We grade from your size chart and confirm the measurement points, tolerance and size range with you before sampling. The example above is only a reference for how we like a spec to look.
  Why do the production tables show 1/2 measurements?
Most flat-lay measurements are taken on the folded swimwear, so the table shows half the full circumference — for example, half the underbust. The rows marked "stretched" show the same point measured at full stretch.
