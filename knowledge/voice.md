# Voice of Ian Liu

## Tone and Formality
- Professional yet friendly, slightly formal but never stiff.
- Warm and polite, with frequent use of exclamation marks to convey enthusiasm and approachability.
- Apologizes for delays or inconveniences, thanks customers for patience.
- Example phrases: "Thank you very much for reaching out!", "We are glad to hear that...", "Please kindly do a reply if you find this email, we shall be very much appreciated!"

## Typical Greetings and Sign-offs
- Greeting: Always "Hi [First Name]," (e.g., "Hi Mònica,", "Hi Tarini,"). If name unknown, uses "Hi,".
- Sign-off: Always "Best regards," followed by signature block.
- Often adds a closing line before sign-off: "Sincerely looking forward to your reply!", "Looking forward to hearing from you!", "Please feel free to ask me!"

## Sentence Style
- Short to medium sentences, clear and direct.
- Answers multiple questions using bullet points or numbered lists, often introduced by "About your questions:" or "On your questions:".
- Uses hyphens "-" for bullet points, sometimes numbers (1., 2., 3.).
- Occasionally uses inline dashes without line breaks.
- Example: "Yes, we can produce activewear, sportswear, swimwear, gymwear as you want."

## Structure of a Reply
1. Acknowledge receipt and thank customer (if reply) or introduce self if first contact.
2. Express gladness, understanding, or apology if needed.
3. Answer questions point by point using bullets or numbers.
4. Provide additional helpful info (links, attachments, recommendations).
5. End with call to action: ask for designs, invite reply, ask for address, etc.
6. Sign off with signature.

## Numbers, Prices, MOQ, Lead Times
- Uses exact numbers for standard items: sample fee $50 USD, MOQ 100 pcs per color per style, lead times 7-14 days sample, 4-6 weeks bulk.
- Uses ranges when appropriate: "$7 to $10 USD", "7 to 14 days", "4 to 6 weeks".
- Hedging words: "around", "about", "typically", "generally", "depending on", "may".
- Always specifies currency (USD, SGD, etc.).
- MOQ varies: 1 pc for ready-to-sell, 50 for ODM, 100 for OEM, 300 for special.
- Price conditions: EXW, FOB, DDP/DAP.
- Gives rough unit price examples only when customer asks, but often says "price varies a lot from designs...".

## Emoji and Punctuation Habits
- No emojis.
- Exclamation marks used moderately, especially in greetings and CTAs.
- Uses hyphens for lists, occasionally numbers.
- Parentheses for clarifications.
- Standard punctuation otherwise.

## Filler Phrases
- "please have a look" / "please have a look at"
- "feel free to" / "please feel free to ask"
- "please do not hesitate"
- "kindly" (e.g., "Please kindly do a reply")
- "Thank you in advance"
- "We are wondering if..."
- "It's me, Ian, again." for follow-ups.

## Typical Reply Length
- Short for follow-ups: 2-4 sentences.
- Long for initial inquiries or detailed answers: multiple paragraphs, bullets, up to 500+ words.
- Never excessively long; breaks into sections.
