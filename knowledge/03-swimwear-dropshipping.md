# Dropshipping

Sell swimwear without holding inventory. You run the store — we pick, pack and ship each order to your customer, with your branding on the packaging.

## Who this is for

Online sellers and new brands who want to sell swimwear without inventory or upfront stock. You run the storefront; we handle picking, packing and shipping for every order. Want to grow beyond single-piece orders? Our private label service lets you buy your own stock at wholesale prices.

## How it works

- **Choose your styles.** Browse our existing styles and confirm with us which ones are available for dropshipping.

- **Confirm stock.** We give you an estimated stock level per variant to enter in your store. Confirm availability before you list a style.

- **Set up your branding.** Send us your own packaging trims (poly bags, hang tags, hygienic liners, stickers, etc.), or use our trim sourcing service — share your design files and we order them for you.

- **We store your trims.** Your trims are kept in our warehouse, marked for your brand and packed with your orders.

- **Share each order.** When a customer orders from your store, send us the details: style number, color, print, size, quantity, shipping address and contact information.

- **Pay and we ship.** Each order is paid individually — product price plus shipping. Once payment is confirmed, we ship and provide tracking.

## Branding rules

**Finished swimwear keeps our generic TPU label.** We don't offer label replacement on completed products. What you can brand is everything that doesn't change the swimwear itself — your own poly bags, hang tags, hygienic liners, stickers, etc., packed with each order.

## Stock & availability

The stock levels we confirm are **estimates, not guarantees** — another customer could place a large order and clear a style at any time.

**Reserve stock to protect your listings.** We recommend reserving a small quantity per variant. Reserved pieces are held in our warehouse and are not sold to anyone else.

## What you should know

- You choose which styles to list. Our range includes our own designs and bestsellers — once a style is sold, we don't take responsibility for how it performs in your store.

- Our dropshipping system is still in development. Orders are shared with us manually today; store integration with real-time stock updates and style requests is on the way.

## Common questions

 Can I use my own label on the swimwear?
No. Finished swimwear carries our standard generic TPU label, and re-labelling isn't offered. Your branding goes on the packaging trims — bags, hang tags, hygienic liners, etc.
  How do I know what is in stock before listing a style?
Ask us before you list. We confirm availability and give you an estimated stock level per variant. Estimates aren't guaranteed — reserving a small quantity per variant is the safest way to protect your listings.
  How is each order paid?
Per order: product price plus shipping. We ship once payment is confirmed and provide tracking for every order.
