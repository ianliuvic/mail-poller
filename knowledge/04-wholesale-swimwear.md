## Wholesale questions

The details buyers usually ask about.

  What is the minimum order quantity?
50 pieces per color per style, with multiple sizes allowed toward the quantity.   Can I use my own labels and packaging?
Yes. We can apply your hang tags, poly bags, hygienic liners and similar trims. Swimwear keeps our standard labels — if you need custom swimwear labels, that falls under private label & ODM.   Can I order a sample first?
Yes. If the style has samples in stock, you can order a sample at the retail price shown on the product page. For styles without stock, sampling costs $50 per style (max 2 pieces per style), refundable when you place a bulk order of 300+ pieces. See sampling for details.   How do payments work?
Bank transfer or Wise. Ready-to-ship wholesale orders are paid in full; ODM/OEM production takes a 30–50% deposit with the balance before shipment. See payment methods for details.   Do you ship worldwide?
Yes. Express (DHL, FedEx, 4PX) or sea freight. EXW, FOB, CIF, DDU and DDP are available — import duties depend on the terms and destination. See logistics for details.
