# OEM Manufacturing

You bring the design — we bring the production line. From drawings, tech packs or a swimwear sample, we develop and manufacture swimwear to your specification.

## Who this is for

OEM is for brands and designers with their own swimwear designs. The style comes from you — we handle development, sampling and production. Your own labels (TPU, heat transfer or woven) are applied at production.

## How it works

- **Send your style.** Share your design drawings, tech pack or swimwear sample. A similar style from our range also helps us judge feasibility.

- **Feasibility check.** We review your style and confirm whether sampling is possible.

- **Pay the sample fee.** $50 per style (sometimes higher), then sampling begins — see our sampling page for details.

- **Sampling.** We develop the sample based on your tech pack or swimwear sample.

- **Sample approval.** You approve the sample — it sets the production reference.

- **Bulk production.** Production runs 28–35 days once your order is confirmed.

## What we need from you

The better the starting point, the faster and more accurate the result. In order of preference:

- ⭐ Sample + tech packPhysical swimwear sample with a complete tech pack — the best starting point.

- 👍 Sample onlyPhysical swimwear sample — we develop the tech pack from it.

- 🙂 Tech pack onlyA complete tech pack — clear and workable without the swimwear.

- 😕 Images or partial packFront/back images or an incomplete tech pack — possible, but development takes more time.

- 🚫 Sketch or conceptStrongly not recommended — aligning the details takes extensive back-and-forth.

A complete tech pack includes front and back images, the fabric and trims list with requirements, flat measurements for each part, and grading rules. Need help specifying or sourcing fabric or trims? See our fabrics & trims sourcing page.

No tech pack? We can still work from images or a swimwear sample — just expect more time in development.

**About AI-generated images.** Renderings that don't account for real fabric and construction fall into the hardest category — matching them takes extensive back-and-forth and is extremely time-consuming. We strongly advise against starting from a sketch or concept alone.

## Sampling first

OEM always starts with a sample — bulk production only begins after you approve it. If you send us a swimwear sample, shipping is on you; let us know whether you want it returned. Samples are checked against your spec — see our quality control guide.

**We may decline sampling** when the order quantity is low, the style is complex, or the information provided is too thin. If we decline, we explain why and suggest alternatives — such as adapting one of our existing styles.

## Your design stays yours

Your patterns, tech packs and styles remain your property. We don't share them with other buyers, and we can work under NDA.
