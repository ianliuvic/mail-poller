# Private Label & ODM

Two ways to make our styles yours. Keep the swimwear as it is and add your brand — or adjust the product itself. Either way, you start from our existing styles.

## Private label vs ODM

Both start the same way: browse our existing styles and choose what fits your line. The difference is how far the product changes.

 | Private Label | ODM
Styles | Our existing styles | Our existing styles
Label | Generic TPU label | Your own label
What is customised | Brand trims only — hang tags, bags, hygienic liners, etc. | Color, print, size, fabric, edge finishing, labels, etc.
Pricing | Wholesale price | Custom production price
MOQ | 50 pcs / style / color | 100 pcs / style / color

## Private label

Keep the swimwear exactly as it is and make the packaging yours. You choose our existing styles and we add your brand trims to the order — at wholesale pricing.

**Swimwear keeps our generic TPU label.** Your branding goes on the trims — hang tags, poly bags, hygienic liners, stickers, etc. If you need to change the swimwear label itself, that moves your order into ODM.

- MOQ & pricingWholesale pricing starts at 50 pieces per style per color.

- Stock & replenishmentWe confirm stock before you order. If a style doesn't have enough, we check whether a replenishment production is possible (subject to capacity) — if it is, lead time follows bulk production; if not, we adjust or cancel.

- Fulfilment · dropshippingWe store your stock and ship orders to your customers at single-piece shipping rates (no volume discount).

- Fulfilment · one-timeYour order ships to your warehouse in one go, with shipping paid once.

- SamplingWant to see the style first? Buy one piece at our single-piece price, plus shipping — handled like a dropshipping order.

## ODM — light customisation

Start from our existing styles and adjust the product itself: color, print, size, fabric, edge finishing, labels, etc. Because each order re-enters production, minimums and pricing differ from private label.

**Your own label is available at this tier.** TPU, heat transfer, woven labels, etc. can be applied as part of your customisation.

Option | Detail
MOQ | 100 pcs / style / color
Bulk production | 28–35 days
Sampling | Samples available before bulk — see our sampling page

Delivery options are the same as private label: warehouse dropshipping or one-time shipment. For custom production we recommend one-time shipment.

## Brand trims for both options

For private label and ODM, you either send us your own brand trims (hang tags, poly bags, hygienic liners, etc.) or use our sourcing service — for sourced trims, share your design files and we order them for you.

## Common questions

 Can I change the label with private label?
No. Private label keeps our generic TPU label; your branding is added through trims. Changing the swimwear label is part of ODM.
  What are the minimums?
Private label: 50 pieces per style per color. ODM: 100 pieces per style per color.
  Can I see a sample before ordering?
Yes. For private label, order one piece at our single-piece price plus shipping. For ODM, samples follow our sampling process — see our sampling page.
  How are brand trims handled?
Send us your own trims, or use our sourcing service and share your design files. Trims need to arrive before production starts.
